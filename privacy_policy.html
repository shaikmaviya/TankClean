Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Learn how TankClean handles your personal information when you use our cleaning services and applications.

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TankClean ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our website, customer application, partner application, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

By using our Services, you acknowledge this policy. Where consent is required by law, we will request it before the relevant information is processed.

This policy should be read together with any privacy notices presented when you create an account or use a particular feature.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

We may collect your name, phone number, account identifier, profile details, and authentication-related information needed to create and manage your account.

Customer booking information

When you make a booking, we may process your service address, tank type, capacity, appointment date and time, booking amount, booking status, service instructions, and relevant communications.

Partner information

For service partners, we may process profile details, phone numbers, account identifiers, assigned bookings, service status, earnings records, payout requests, and payout details needed to administer partner services.

Payment information

We may retain transaction references, payment amount, payment method, booking reference, payment status, and refund or payout records. Payment providers may separately process information required to complete transactions.

Location information

We may process service addresses and associated coordinates provided for a booking. If a feature requests device location, location information is collected only as permitted by your device settings and the applicable permissions.

Photos and uploaded content

Where supported, photos uploaded by customers or partners may be processed to document tank condition, service progress, completion, or a support issue.

Technical information

Depending on the features and software components enabled, we may process app version, device and operating-system details, notification tokens, diagnostic information, and security logs.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Create and manage customer and partner accounts.
  • Schedule bookings and coordinate cleaning services.
  • Share relevant customer and appointment details with the assigned partner.
  • Process and verify payments and administer partner payouts.
  • Provide booking, payment, assignment, and completion updates.
  • Handle customer support requests, complaints, and disputes.
  • Maintain platform security and help prevent fraud or misuse.
  • Improve the reliability of our Services.
  • Maintain necessary records and comply with legal obligations.

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, processing may be based on providing a requested service, fulfilling an agreement, complying with law, legitimate interests, or consent.

3. How We Share Information

Customers and assigned partners

To complete a booking, the assigned partner may receive the customer's name, phone number, service address, appointment details, and other information necessary for the visit. Customers may receive relevant partner details for coordination.

Service providers

We may share information with providers that support our database, authentication, payments, hosting, maps, file storage, or notification services.

Legal and safety purposes

We may disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, investigate fraud, protect users, resolve disputes, or safeguard our rights and systems.

Business transfers

Information may be transferred as part of a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or transfer of business assets, subject to applicable privacy requirements.

We do not sell personal information as a business model.

4. Payments and Partner Payouts

TankClean may use Razorpay to process online payments. Payment details required to complete a transaction may be handled by the payment provider under its own terms and privacy policy.

TankClean may retain transaction identifiers, payment amounts, booking references, and payment status for confirmation, reconciliation, customer support, and dispute resolution.

For partners, we may maintain payout amounts and payout statuses, such as pending, paid, or rejected. A payout status should only be marked paid after the transfer has been verified.

5. Location and Service Addresses

Service addresses help partners reach the correct location. Coordinates may be used for booking or navigation features where supported.

  • Provide an address that is necessary for your requested service.
  • You can manage device location permissions in your device settings.
  • Saved addresses may remain associated with bookings where needed for service or recordkeeping.

This policy does not claim that TankClean continuously tracks your location. Any background location feature would require separate review and appropriate disclosures and permissions.

6. Photos and Uploaded Files

Where photo uploads are supported, images may document tank condition, cleaning progress, service completion, or a complaint. Files may be stored with our configured storage provider and linked to a relevant booking.

Access should be limited to authorized users who need the information for service delivery, support, or legitimate operational purposes. Please avoid uploading unrelated sensitive information.

7. Notifications and App Permissions

TankClean may send push notifications about booking confirmation, partner assignment, payment verification, service completion, and important service updates.

Firebase Cloud Messaging may process a device or app token to deliver notifications. You can disable notifications in your device settings, although you may miss service updates.

Depending on the features you use, the app may request location, camera, or photo-library permissions. You can manage these permissions in your device settings.

8. Data Storage and Security

TankClean uses Supabase for configured backend services, which may include database storage, authentication, and file storage. Our other service providers may process data in locations determined by their infrastructure and settings.

We aim to protect information through appropriate measures, including:

  • Access controls for accounts and administrative functions.
  • Database and storage permissions.
  • Secure network connections where supported.
  • Appropriate handling of payment credentials and API secrets.
  • Measures to investigate suspected unauthorized access.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely risk-free.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain booking and payment records, handle disputes, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary by data type. When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete it or de-identify it, subject to legal requirements, backups, and legitimate recordkeeping needs.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may be able to request access to your information, correction of inaccurate details, deletion of eligible information, withdrawal of consent, or assistance with a privacy complaint.

We may need to verify your identity before processing a request. Certain information may need to be retained where required or permitted by law.

11. Account and Data Deletion

You may request account deletion and deletion of eligible associated personal information by contacting TankClean using the contact details below. Include the phone number associated with your account and state that you want your account deleted.

We may verify your identity before processing the request. Eligible data will be deleted or de-identified in accordance with applicable requirements.

Some booking, transaction, fraud-prevention, dispute, or legally required records may need to be retained for a permitted period. Deleting an account does not necessarily remove every record immediately.

If you offer account creation in your apps, make sure the deletion process described here is actually available and complies with applicable Google Play requirements.

12. Children's Privacy

TankClean is intended for people who can legally arrange the relevant services. We do not intend to knowingly collect children's personal information in a manner prohibited by law.

If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the matter.

14. Applicable Law

TankClean intends to handle personal information in accordance with applicable Indian privacy and data protection laws, including relevant provisions of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and applicable rules and commencement notifications as in force from time to time.

The rights and obligations that apply depend on the relevant law and processing activity.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our services, technology, business practices, or legal requirements. We will update the date at the top when changes are made and provide additional notice or obtain consent where required by law.
