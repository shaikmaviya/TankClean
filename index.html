Frequently Asked Questions

Questions? We've got you.

Some helpful information before arranging your service.

How can I book a tank-cleaning service?

Choose an available service in the TankClean customer app, enter your service details, and follow the booking confirmation process. You can also contact our support team.

Which types of tanks can I enquire about?

You can enquire about overhead tanks, underground sumps, plastic tanks, RCC tanks, and commercial tanks. Confirm availability for your specific tank before booking.

How do I check my booking status?

Check the bookings section of the TankClean customer app and review the latest status available for your booking.

How can I contact customer support?

Call our support number at 7780113910 for assistance with bookings and service enquiries.

How often should a water tank be cleaned?

The appropriate cleaning frequency depends on the tank, water source, usage, and local conditions. Inspect your tank regularly and arrange cleaning when needed.
